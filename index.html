Authorized Municipal Collection Centres

Metro Recycling Hub

Location: 101 Innovation Way, Sector 4

Accepted Devices: Laptops Mobile Phones

Schedule: Mon-Fri (08:00 AM - 05:00 PM)

Instructions: Remove battery cells before drop-off; wipe personal local storage.

Processing Status: Operational

Downtown Green Depot

Location: 404 Maple Ave, Civic District

Accepted Devices: Televisions Batteries

Schedule: Sat-Sun (10:00 AM - 04:00 PM)

Instructions: Tape battery contacts securely to eliminate combustion risks during routing.

Processing Status: Sorting Batch Alpha

Silicon Valley Eco-Yard

Location: 77 Tech Boulevard, Industrial Zone

Accepted Devices: Laptops Televisions Mobile Phones

Schedule: Mon-Thu (09:00 AM - 06:00 PM)

Instructions: Intact casing required. Heavily cracked screen assemblies require safe-wrap bins.

Processing Status: Extracting Noble Metals

Suburban Battery Collection Point

Location: 12 Pine Road, Residential North

Accepted Devices: Batteries

Schedule: Everyday (24/7 Drop-box)

Instructions: Limited to lithium-ion, NiMH, and alkaline consumer cells.

Processing Status: Awaiting Hazardous Freight Transport

Westside Electronics Reclamation Facility

Location: 890 Industrial Express, Gate 2

Accepted Devices: Laptops Batteries Televisions

Schedule: Mon-Fri (07:00 AM - 04:00 PM)

Instructions: Bulk enterprise configurations require validation certificates before intake.

Processing Status: Shredding and Smelting Baseline
